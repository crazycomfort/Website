Why Work at Crazy Comfort?

We built this company because we were tired of the way HVAC was being done. If you feel the same way, you might belong here.

No Sales Pressure

We don't push unnecessary repairs. If it doesn't need fixing, we don't fix it. Your job is to diagnose honestly and explain clearly—not hit quotas.

Family-Run, Not Corporate

We're a small, tight-knit team. You'll know everyone by name. Decisions happen fast. Your voice matters here.

Reasonable Hours

Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm. Weekends are emergency-only and voluntary. We believe in work-life balance—not burnout.

Do Things Right

We document everything. No shortcuts. No "good enough." If you take pride in doing quality work, you'll fit right in.

Competitive Pay

Fair compensation based on your skills and experience. We pay for quality work, not upselling ability.

Professional Growth

We invest in training and education. Learn new skills, stay current with technology, and grow your career with us.

Who We're Looking For

HVAC Service Technician

We're seeking experienced HVAC technicians who value quality over speed and honesty over sales.

What You'll Do:

  • ✓ Diagnose and repair residential and light commercial HVAC systems
  • ✓ Document every visit with photos and video (we provide the tools)
  • ✓ Explain findings clearly to homeowners—no jargon, no pressure
  • ✓ Perform maintenance, repairs, and installations to manufacturer specs
  • ✓ Represent Crazy Comfort with professionalism and integrity

What We're Looking For:

  • → 3+ years HVAC experience (residential preferred)
  • → EPA 608 Universal Certification
  • → Valid Texas driver's license with clean record
  • → Strong diagnostic skills and attention to detail
  • → Ability to communicate clearly with customers
  • → Pride in doing quality work—every time

Bonus points: NATE certification, heat pump experience, mini-split installation experience, or commercial HVAC background.

Don't see a perfect fit? We're always open to meeting skilled professionals. Send us your information and we'll keep you in mind for future opportunities.
