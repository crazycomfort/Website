Why Work at Crazy Comfort?

We built this company because we were tired of the way HVAC was being done. If you feel the same way, you might belong here.

Honest Work, Real Earnings

We run a real business with real targets—that's how we succeed. But we never push unnecessary repairs. Good techs who do honest, quality work will earn well here.

Family-Run, Not Corporate

We're a small, tight-knit team. You'll know everyone by name. Decisions happen fast. Your voice matters here.

Reasonable Hours

Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm. Weekends are emergency-only and voluntary. We believe in work-life balance—not burnout.

Do Things Right

We document everything. No shortcuts. No "good enough." If you take pride in doing quality work, you'll fit right in.

Earn What You're Worth

Commission-based pay with daily targets. We know what it takes to run a successful company—and we reward techs who produce. Skilled, honest work pays well here.

Paid Training & Onboarding

Every hire goes through thorough paid onboarding to learn the Crazy Comfort way. We invest in training so you're set up to succeed from day one.

Who We're Looking For

HVAC Service Technician

We're seeking experienced HVAC technicians who value quality over speed and honesty over sales.

What You'll Do:

  • ✓ Diagnose and repair residential and light commercial HVAC systems
  • ✓ Document every visit with photos and video (we provide the tools)
  • ✓ Communicate findings clearly to homeowners using our proven system
  • ✓ Perform maintenance, repairs, and installations to manufacturer specs
  • ✓ Represent Crazy Comfort with professionalism and integrity

What We're Looking For:

  • → 3+ years HVAC experience (residential preferred)
  • → EPA 608 Universal Certification
  • → Valid Texas driver's license with clean record
  • → Strong diagnostic skills and attention to detail
  • → Ability to communicate clearly with customers
  • → Pride in doing quality work—every time

Bonus points: NATE certification, heat pump experience, mini-split installation experience, or commercial HVAC background.

HVAC Apprentice / Helper

New to HVAC or looking to grow your skills? We'll train the right person. Must be willing to learn, work hard, and show up on time.

  • → Some mechanical aptitude or trade school background preferred
  • → Valid Texas driver's license
  • → Reliable transportation and strong work ethic

Office / Customer Service

Help us deliver exceptional customer experiences. Answer calls, schedule appointments, and keep our operations running smoothly.

  • → Excellent phone and communication skills
  • → Comfortable with scheduling software and computers
  • → Organized, friendly, and detail-oriented
