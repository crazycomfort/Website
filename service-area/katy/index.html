HVAC Challenges for Katy Homes

Katy's rapid growth means a mix of brand-new construction and established neighborhoods, each with unique HVAC needs. Whether you're in a master-planned community like Cinco Ranch or an older neighborhood near downtown Katy, we understand the specific challenges your home faces.

Common Issues We See in Katy:

  • New construction settling: Ductwork can shift as new homes settle, causing airflow problems and uneven cooling
  • High humidity: Katy's proximity to Houston means humidity control is essential for comfort and preventing mold
  • Oversized systems: Many Katy homes have HVAC systems that are too large, leading to short cycling and humidity issues
