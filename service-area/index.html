Service Area Questions

What areas does Crazy Comfort serve?
We provide HVAC services throughout Katy, Fulshear, Richmond, Sugar Land, Cinco Ranch, Brookshire, Simonton, Sealy, and surrounding West Houston communities. Our primary focus is Katy and Fulshear, but we regularly serve the entire West Houston area. Zip codes include 77449, 77450, 77493, 77494, 77441, 77406, 77407, 77469, 77478, 77479, 77498, 77423, 77476, and 77474.
Do you charge extra for service calls outside Katy?
No, we do not charge extra trip fees for any of our primary service areas. Whether you're in Katy, Fulshear, Richmond, Sugar Land, Cinco Ranch, Brookshire, Simonton, or Sealy, you receive the same pricing. Our diagnostic fee is $129 for residential systems, applied toward repair if you proceed.
Is same-day HVAC service available in my area?
Yes, same-day service is available for most areas we serve, subject to technician availability. Maintenance Plan members receive priority scheduling and are served ahead of non-members when scheduling allows. During peak summer months, priority scheduling can mean the difference between same-day and next-day service.
Do you service Cinco Ranch?
Yes, Cinco Ranch is one of our most-served areas. We provide AC repair, heating repair, maintenance plans, system replacement, and ductwork services throughout all Cinco Ranch neighborhoods and villages. We're familiar with the builders, home styles, and common HVAC systems in this community.
What zip codes do you serve in Katy TX?
We serve all Katy zip codes including 77449, 77450, 77493, and 77494. This includes neighborhoods like Cinco Ranch, Seven Meadows, Firethorne, Cane Island, Tamarron, Grand Lakes, Pine Mill Ranch, Nottingham Country, Kelliwood, Memorial Parkway, and all surrounding Katy communities.
Do you offer emergency HVAC service in Fulshear?
Yes, we offer same-day and priority HVAC service in Fulshear. Fulshear is one of our primary service areas, and we're familiar with the homes, builders, and common HVAC systems in Cross Creek Ranch, Fulbrook, Fulbrook on Fulshear Creek, Weston Lakes, and other Fulshear communities.
How far west of Houston do you provide service?
We service communities as far west as Sealy, including Brookshire and Simonton. Our coverage extends from West Houston through Katy, Fulshear, Richmond, and Sugar Land. We provide both residential and commercial HVAC service in Sealy and surrounding areas. If you're unsure whether we serve your specific location, call us at 832-403-7466 and we'll let you know.
Do you offer commercial HVAC service?
Yes, we provide commercial HVAC services throughout the Houston area. Visit our Commercial HVAC Services page for details on our commercial capabilities, the types of businesses we serve, and our current commercial clients.
What makes Crazy Comfort different from other HVAC companies in Katy?
We document every service call with real-time photos and video—timestamped and GPS-stamped so you know it's your home, your system, right now. Not stock photos. Not someone else's job. We're woman-owned, family-operated, and we never recommend repairs without showing you documented evidence of the problem first. Our technicians have nearly 30 years of combined experience.

Not Sure If We Serve Your Area?

Give us a call. If you're anywhere in the West Houston area, there's a good chance we can help. No pressure, just answers.
