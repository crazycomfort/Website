Common Questions

Will checking my rate hurt my credit score?
No. Hearth uses a soft credit inquiry to show you rates and options. This does NOT affect your credit score. A hard inquiry only happens if you choose to proceed with an offer.
What credit score do I need?
Hearth works with multiple lenders who have different requirements. Generally, a score of 600+ gives you a good chance of approval. Even with lower scores, there may be options available—it's worth checking.
How long does approval take?
Most customers see their options within minutes. The application takes about 60 seconds to complete. If approved, you can sign documents digitally and we can often install same-day or next-day.
Can I pay off my loan early?
Yes! There are no prepayment penalties. Pay off your balance early and save on interest—no extra fees, no fine print games.
What if I'm not approved?
No problem. We can discuss other options like phased repairs, alternative lenders, or priority scheduling once you're ready. We're not going to leave you without AC in Texas—we'll figure something out.
Is there a minimum or maximum financing amount?
Minimums and maximums vary by lender. Generally, financing is available for projects starting around $1,000 and up to $100,000. Most residential HVAC projects fall well within this range.
