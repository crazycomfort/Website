AC Maintenance Plans

1. What This Service Is

AC maintenance is a scheduled inspection and cleaning of the cooling system to keep airflow, drainage, electrical components, and refrigerant circuit within normal ranges. It prevents faults by finding issues early.

2. Common Symptoms or Triggers

  • Rising energy bills without changes in usage
  • Reduced airflow or uneven temperatures
  • Musty odors at startup
  • Water near the air handler or intermittent drain clogs
  • System short-cycles or runs longer than usual
  • Visible dust buildup on returns or vents

3. How We Diagnose (Critical)

  1. Verify thermostat operation and system start/stop.
  2. Inspect filter, returns, and supply airflow; note restrictions.
  3. Inspect and clean indoor coil area (as accessible), drain pan, and drain line; test drainage.
  4. Check blower wheel and motor for cleanliness, rotation, and amp draw.
  5. Inspect electrical components (capacitors, contactor, wiring) for tolerance and condition.
  6. Inspect outdoor coil and fan operation; clear debris and check clearances.
  7. Measure refrigerant pressures/temps when conditions allow to confirm operating range.
  8. Document all findings with photos/video and note any deviations from normal.

4. Options We May Recommend

  • Replace or clean the air filter and address return restrictions.
  • Clear and treat the condensate drain if buildup is present.
  • Clean blower wheel and indoor/outdoor coils if impacted with debris.
  • Replace electrical components that test out of specification.
  • Address airflow issues (duct obstructions, balancing) if confirmed by measurement.
  • Plan further inspection or repair if pressures/temps indicate a refrigerant-side issue.

5. What We Will NOT Sell

  • No chemical treatments or coil cleanings unless buildup is documented.
  • No electrical part replacements without test results showing failure or out-of-spec readings.
  • No duct or airflow changes without measured evidence (static pressure/visual blockage).
  • No refrigerant adjustments without cause identified.

6. FAQs

  • How often should I service my AC? Typically twice a year in our climate—before heavy cooling and before heating if using a heat pump.
  • Do you add refrigerant during maintenance? Only if testing shows a charge issue and the cause is identified; maintenance is inspection and cleaning.
  • What if my filter looks clean? We still check size, fit, and airflow; a clean filter can still be too restrictive for the system.
  • Do you clean coils every visit? We clean or wash when buildup is present; we do not apply treatments without need.
  • Will maintenance stop all breakdowns? It reduces risk by catching issues early, but mechanical parts can still fail.
  • Do you check drains? Yes, we inspect and clear/treat if there is evidence of buildup or slow drainage.

7. Evidence Block

During maintenance we document filters, coils, drains, electrical tests, and airflow observations with photos or video. We explain any deviations before suggesting corrective work so you can “See What We See.”
