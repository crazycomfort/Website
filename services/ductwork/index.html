Ductwork Cleaning, Repair & Installation

1. What This Service Is

Ductwork service addresses the air distribution system: cleaning to remove buildup, sealing or repairing leaks, and replacing or adding ducts when condition or sizing requires it. The goal is to restore designed airflow paths and cleanliness.

2. Common Symptoms or Triggers

  • Rooms that are consistently hotter or colder
  • Visible dust discharge from vents
  • Musty odors when the system runs
  • Whistling or rattling noises from ducts
  • Air handler running long with poor airflow
  • Known duct damage, gaps, or disconnected runs

3. How We Diagnose (Critical)

  1. Inspect visible duct runs for damage, kinks, disconnections, and insulation condition.
  2. Check plenums, takeoffs, and boots for gaps, air leaks, and contamination.
  3. Assess return and supply sizes; look for obvious restrictions or crushed flex.
  4. Observe airflow at supplies/returns; note rooms with poor delivery.
  5. Check filter fit and cleanliness; note bypass or incorrect size.
  6. Document findings with photos/video before recommending cleaning, sealing, or replacement.

4. Options We May Recommend

  • Duct cleaning when buildup is present and accessible.
  • Sealing joints, boots, and accessible leaks when confirmed.
  • Repairing or replacing damaged or undersized sections if documented.
  • Adding or adjusting returns/supplies if airflow is insufficient and space allows.
  • Insulation repairs where missing or compromised.

5. What We Will NOT Sell

  • No duct replacements without visible damage or airflow evidence.
  • No sealing claims without confirmed leaks.
  • No cleaning if ducts are clean/accessible areas show no buildup.
  • No add-on products unrelated to documented duct issues.

6. FAQs

  • How do I know if ducts need cleaning? Visible debris at registers, confirmed buildup inside accessible sections, or documented contamination.
  • Can sealing fix uneven rooms? Only if leaks or design issues are the cause; we document findings before sealing.
  • Do you replace all ducts by default? No. Replacement is limited to damaged, improperly sized, or failing sections.
  • Will cleaning damage the ducts? We use methods appropriate to duct type and condition; we do not clean if it risks damage.
  • Do you add vents to fix airflow? Only when sizing and layout support it and a restriction is documented.
  • What if only one room is affected? We inspect that branch for kinks, disconnections, or sizing issues before recommending changes.

7. Evidence Block

We document duct condition, airflow observations, and any leaks or blockages with photos or video. Recommendations for cleaning, sealing, or replacement are made only after you can “See What We See.”
