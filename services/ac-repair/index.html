AC Repair in Katy & Fulshear

1. What This Service Is

AC repair is the process of finding and correcting faults in a cooling system so it can cool, dehumidify, and circulate air as designed. It focuses on restoring function, not upgrading equipment.

2. Common Symptoms or Triggers

  • Unit runs but air is warm or airflow is weak
  • System short-cycles or won’t start
  • Water around the air handler or ceiling stains
  • Ice on the indoor coil or refrigerant lines
  • Unusual noises (buzzing, rattling, grinding)
  • Breaker trips when the system starts
  • Thermostat calls for cooling but no response

3. How We Diagnose (Critical)

  1. Verify thermostat settings and power to the system.
  2. Inspect air filter, return grilles, and supply airflow.
  3. Check indoor coil, drain pan, and drain line for blockage or overflow.
  4. Measure electrical components (capacitors, contactor, wiring) for tolerance and operation.
  5. Inspect blower wheel and motor for rotation, noise, and amp draw.
  6. Inspect outdoor unit: fan operation, coil condition, debris, and clearances.
  7. Measure refrigerant pressures/temps (when conditions allow) to assess charge and system health.
  8. Document findings with photos/video; confirm observations before recommending work.

4. Options We May Recommend

  • Clean/replace air filter and clear return obstructions.
  • Clear condensate drain and restore proper drainage.
  • Replace failed electrical components (e.g., capacitor, contactor) if testing shows out-of-spec.
  • Address airflow issues (blower cleaning, duct obstructions) if confirmed.
  • Correct low/high refrigerant conditions only after leak/source is identified and documented.
  • Recommend further inspection or seal/repair if coil, line set, or major components show confirmed defects.

5. What We Will NOT Sell

  • No refrigerant “top-offs” without identifying cause.
  • No component replacements without test results showing failure.
  • No duct or coil replacements without visual evidence and airflow/pressure findings.
  • No add-ons or upsells unrelated to the documented issue.

6. FAQs

  • Why is the AC running but not cooling? Often airflow restriction, refrigerant issue, or a failed outdoor component; we test each part before recommending a fix.
  • Why is there ice on the lines or coil? Usually low airflow or low charge; we check filter, blower, coil condition, and pressures to confirm.
  • Why is water leaking at the air handler? A clogged drain or pan overflow; we clear and verify drainage and safety switches.
  • Should I keep running it if it’s not cooling? If ice or abnormal noise is present, turn it off to prevent further damage until inspected.
  • How do you decide a part is bad? We measure against manufacturer specs (capacitance, amp draw, continuity, voltage) and document results.
  • Do you add refrigerant without finding a leak? No; we only correct charge after identifying the cause and obtaining approval.
  • What if the problem is intermittent? We document current readings, look for patterns (cycles, temperatures, pressures), and may recommend monitoring or follow-up testing.

7. Evidence Block

Every AC repair visit is documented with photos or video of the findings (filters, coils, drains, electrical tests, pressures when taken). We explain what we see before recommending any correction so you can “See What We See.”
