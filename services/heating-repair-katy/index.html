Signs Your Katy Heater Needs Repair

🔥

No Heat at All

Furnace runs but blows cold air, or won't turn on at all. Could be ignitor, gas valve, or control board.

🔄

Short Cycling

Heater turns on and off frequently without reaching temperature. Often a safety issue.

👃

Strange Smells

Burning smells or gas odors require immediate attention. Turn off your system and call us.

🔊

Loud Noises

Banging, squealing, or rumbling sounds indicate mechanical problems that need diagnosis.
