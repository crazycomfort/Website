Common AC Problems in Richmond

AC Running But Not Cooling

System runs constantly but home stays warm. Could be refrigerant leak, compressor issue, or airflow problem.

Ice on AC Lines

Ice formation indicates low refrigerant or restricted airflow. Turn off system and call us immediately.

Water Leaking Inside

Condensate drain blockages are common in humid Houston-area weather. We clear drains and prevent damage.

AC Won't Turn On

Could be thermostat, capacitor, contactor, or breaker issue. We diagnose the root cause.
