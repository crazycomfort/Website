Signs Your Katy AC Needs Repair

Texas summers push AC systems hard. If you're experiencing any of these issues, call us for a diagnosis.

AC Running But Not Cooling

Your system runs constantly but the house stays warm. Could be refrigerant, airflow, or compressor issues.

Ice on AC Lines or Coil

Ice formation indicates low airflow or refrigerant problems. Turn off your system and call us immediately.

Water Leaking Inside

Condensate drain blockages are common in humid Katy weather. We clear drains and prevent water damage.

Strange Noises or Smells

Grinding, squealing, or burning smells mean something's wrong. Don't ignore these warning signs.
