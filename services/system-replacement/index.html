Signs It May Be Time for a New System

Not every breakdown means replacement. Here are legitimate reasons we may recommend a new system—each must be documented.

Age & Performance

  • Equipment beyond typical service life (15-20 years)
  • Declining performance despite maintenance
  • Rising energy bills compared to prior seasons

Major Component Failure

  • Failed compressor or heat exchanger
  • Major refrigerant leaks or obsolete refrigerant (R-22)
  • Repair cost exceeds 50% of replacement value

Comfort Problems

  • Uneven temperatures that can't be corrected
  • System too small or large for current needs
  • Frequent breakdowns or repeated part failures

Our Replacement Process

From initial assessment to final testing, every step is documented so you know exactly what's happening.

1

Assessment & Documentation

We inspect your existing equipment (model, serial, condition, age), check duct condition and sizing, and document any leaks, corrosion, safety concerns, or failed components with photos.

2

Load Calculation & Sizing

We consider your home's square footage, insulation, windows, and layout to recommend properly sized equipment. Oversized systems short-cycle and waste energy; undersized systems can't keep up.

3

Equipment Selection

We present options based on your needs and budget—efficiency levels, features, and warranty coverage. We explain the differences so you can make an informed decision.

4

Professional Installation

Complete removal of old equipment, installation of new matched system, and any necessary updates to electrical, line sets, or condensate management. Every step is documented.

5

Testing & Handoff

System startup, testing, and verification of proper operation. We register your warranty, walk you through the thermostat, and provide documentation of the entire installation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you decide on system size?
We consider your home's square footage, insulation levels, window exposure, and existing duct capacity. Oversized equipment short-cycles (turns on and off frequently), wastes energy, and doesn't dehumidify properly. We avoid oversizing.
Do you reuse existing line sets?
Only if condition and compatibility allow. We inspect the existing line set for damage, proper sizing, and cleanliness. If it's compatible with the new system and in good condition, reusing it can save money. Otherwise, we replace it.
What about my existing ductwork?
We inspect your ducts and repair or modify them only if airflow measurements or visible condition requires it. We don't change ducts without cause—but if your ducts are undersized, damaged, or leaking, we'll document the issues and explain your options.
How long does a system replacement take?
A standard split system replacement typically takes one day. More complex jobs involving ductwork modifications, electrical upgrades, or difficult access may take longer. We'll give you a realistic timeline during your estimate.
Do you handle permits and disposal?
Yes. We pull required permits, schedule inspections when applicable, and handle proper disposal of old equipment including refrigerant recovery. It's all included in our quote.
What brands do you install?
We install quality equipment from trusted manufacturers. During your consultation, we'll discuss options that fit your needs and budget—different efficiency levels, features, and warranty terms.
Do you offer financing?
We can discuss payment options during your consultation. Our focus is on providing the right solution for your home—not pushing you toward options that don't fit your budget.
