Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does Crazy Comfort serve?
We provide HVAC services throughout Katy, Fulshear, Richmond, Sugar Land, Cinco Ranch, Brookshire, Simonton, Sealy, and surrounding West Houston communities. Our primary focus is Katy and Fulshear, but we regularly serve the entire West Houston area. View our full service area →
Do you offer same-day HVAC service?
Yes, same-day service is available for most HVAC repairs in our service area. Maintenance Plan members receive priority scheduling and are served ahead of non-members when scheduling allows. Call us at 832-403-7466 to check availability.
What makes Crazy Comfort different from other HVAC companies?
We document every service call with real-time photos and video—timestamped and GPS-stamped so you know it's your home, your system, right now. Not stock photos. Not someone else's job. We never recommend repairs without showing you documented evidence of the problem first. Learn more about our approach →
How much does AC repair cost in Katy TX?
AC repair costs vary based on the issue. Our diagnostic fee is $129 for residential systems, which is applied toward the repair if you proceed. We provide upfront, flat-rate pricing before any work begins, and you'll see documented evidence of the problem before approving repairs. No surprises.
Is Crazy Comfort licensed and insured?
Yes, Crazy Comfort is fully licensed by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) and carries comprehensive liability and workers' compensation insurance. We're woman-owned and family-operated with nearly 30 years of combined HVAC experience between our technicians.
