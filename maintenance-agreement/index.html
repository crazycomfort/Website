One Plan. One Clear Promise. Zero Guesswork.

At Crazy Comfort, we believe HVAC maintenance should be simple, honest, and easy to understand. Why One Thorough Visit Works Better

Instead of rushing through seasonal checklists, we focus on one thorough, professional system review. This visit is scheduled strategically based on your system, usage, and local climate to provide meaningful insight into safety, performance, and overall system health.

Your maintenance visit covers both heating and cooling components and includes a clear system health report with photo documentation, so you know exactly what condition your system is in and what to plan for next.

If you ever want an additional maintenance visit later in the year, you can request one at the same member rate. No pressure. No upgrades. Just professional service when you want it.

Agreement Terms

This Maintenance Agreement includes one comprehensive HVAC system maintenance service during the twelve-month coverage period. The included service covers inspection, evaluation, and preventative maintenance tasks for both heating and cooling components as determined appropriate by Crazy Comfort based on system condition, accessibility, and manufacturer guidelines.

The Maintenance Agreement does not guarantee the prevention of system failure or eliminate the possibility of emergency service needs.

Additional maintenance visits beyond the single included annual service are not included and may be scheduled at the customer's request at the prevailing Maintenance Agreement member rate.

This agreement establishes an annual service relationship and associated member benefits and does not constitute a warranty or insurance policy.
