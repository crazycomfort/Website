# Technician Opening Script

## Deliverable 2: First Walkthrough Video Script

Hi, this is [Technician Name] with Crazy Comfort. I am here at your home for your service visit today.

I am currently in your [attic/equipment room/basement/wherever they are]. I am about to begin the inspection and walkthrough of your HVAC system.

Before I start, I want to make sure you know exactly what is happening. Everything I see, I am documenting with photos and video in real time. These photos and videos are time stamped and location stamped, and they are uploading to the cloud right now as I take them.

You should be receiving a live link to follow along with me. You can watch from your phone, your tablet, or your computer, whether you are here at home or somewhere else entirely. Every photo I take will appear on your device instantly. Every video I record will be shared with you live.

This means you can see exactly what I see, as I see it. You can ask me questions at any time. If something looks concerning to you, or if you want me to explain what I am looking at, just let me know. I will stop and walk you through it.

I am not going to disappear into this space and come back with a recommendation you have to trust. You are invited into every step of this process. You are seeing the same things I am seeing, at the same time I am seeing them.

This documentation becomes part of your permanent service history with us. You will have access to all of these photos and videos for as long as you own this home. Nothing gets lost. Nothing gets forgotten. Everything is recorded.

So if you are ready, I am going to start the walkthrough now. Feel free to follow along on your device, and please do not hesitate to stop me if you have any questions or if you want me to explain anything I am looking at.

Let us begin.
