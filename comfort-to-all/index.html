Comfort To All

& To All A Comfortable Life

Comfort should not be a luxury.

Everyone deserves a safe, comfortable home. Through Comfort To All, we provide free heating and cooling assistance to families in our community who are facing hardship.

What Is Comfort To All?

Comfort To All is our commitment to the community. A portion of every service call goes toward helping families who can't afford heating or cooling repairs. When we identify—or when you nominate—a family in need, we step in with free repairs or equipment.

This isn't marketing. This is responsibility.

We've seen families struggling without AC in Texas summers and without heat in winter. If we have the skills to help, we should.

Who Can Be Nominated?

We prioritize those who need it most. Typical recipients include:

Elderly on Fixed Income

Seniors who struggle to afford basic home repairs.

Single-Parent Households

Families stretching every dollar to make ends meet.

Families Facing Medical Hardship

Those dealing with illness and mounting medical bills.

Veterans in Need

Those who served our country and need support.

We review each nomination individually. If someone you know is struggling, tell us their story—we'll do what we can.

Nominate Someone

Know someone who could use help with their heating or cooling? Fill out this form and we'll review the nomination.

We review every nomination. Due to limited resources, we can't help everyone, but we do our best.

How It Works

1

You Nominate

Fill out the form above with details about someone who needs help. Include their situation and what's wrong with their HVAC.

2

We Review

Our team reviews each nomination. We may contact you or the nominee for more information.

3

We Help

If selected, we schedule a visit, diagnose the issue, and provide the repair or equipment at no cost to the family.

Want to Help Fund Comfort To All?

Every service call you book with Crazy Comfort contributes to this initiative. The best way to support Comfort To All is to use our services and spread the word.
